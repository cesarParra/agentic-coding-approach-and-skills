broom is on. A comment is one of: a link, a platform trap, a regex too dense to read, a directive's one-line reason, a public contract. Two lines at most. Nothing else: not what the code does, why it exists, what it replaced, what a neighbour does, or what sits "below". A comment that could be a name becomes the name. Markdown carries no asides to the reader and no intensifiers. A judge with no context reads every edit, and its verdict is applied, never argued.
