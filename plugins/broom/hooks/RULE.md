broom is on. A comment is one of: a link, a platform trap, a regex too dense to read, a directive's one-line reason, a public contract. Two lines at most. Nothing else: not what the code does, why it exists, what it replaced, what a neighbour does, or what sits "below". A comment that could be a name becomes the name. Markdown carries no asides to the reader and no intensifiers. A judge with no context reads every edit, and its verdict is applied, never argued. A title is an index entry — a commit subject, a pull request or issue title, a task title — read beside thirty others with the body closed: lead with the verb, name the thing by its real identifier, say what happens to it. No metaphor, no problem stated as an observation, no clause that needs the body open. The same judge reads it at the door.
